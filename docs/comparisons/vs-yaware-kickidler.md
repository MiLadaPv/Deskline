# Deskline vs Yaware / Kickidler

CIS buyers often compare “картина дня” tools with heavy monitoring suites.

| | Deskline | Yaware | Kickidler-class |
|--|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Focus timeline | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Live screen video | No | Varies | Strong |
| DLP / keylog | No (by design) | Varies | Often yes |
| Data residency | Local-first | Often cloud/on-prem suite | Suite |

**Choose Deskline** for trust-friendly focus tracking and small-team LAN hub.  
**Choose them** for security investigations, video walls, or formal workforce control.
