# Deskline Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** AndalusGames

**Contact:** milanochka.llc@gmail.com  
**Jurisdiction:** Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan  

Last updated: 2026-07-26

## Summary

Deskline is **local-first**. Your activity data (apps, sites, focus reports, optional screenshots) is stored on your computer under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Deskline`. We do **not** operate a mandatory cloud that receives your activity timeline.

## What we collect on your device

- Active window / browser tab metadata (app name, title, site host) for time tracking  
- Optional screenshots (Pro / Team / trial), if you enable them  
- Local settings, projects/tasks, and license status file  

## What we do not collect

- Keystrokes, clipboard contents, or microphone audio  
- Stealth remote monitoring without your knowledge  
- Continuous upload of activity to AndalusGames servers  

## License / payment

If you purchase Pro or Team via Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record), license **activation and periodic validation** may contact Lemon Squeezy’s API with your license key. That is separate from activity tracking.

## Chrome extension

The extension stores browser focus totals in `chrome.storage` on your machine.  
It may POST closed tab segments to `http://127.0.0.1:8787` **only when Deskline Desktop is running locally**.

## Company / Team LAN hub

Team mode can receive session summaries from other PCs on your LAN using ingest tokens you control. You are responsible for lawful notice to employees and compliance with local employment/privacy law.

## Your controls

- Pause tracking anytime  
- Clear local data from Settings (where provided)  
- Uninstall the app / extension to stop collection
