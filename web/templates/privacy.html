Privacy Policy

How Deskline handles information. Last updated: {{ copyright_year }}. Contact: {{ support_email }}.

1. Who we are

Deskline is provided by {{ company_name }} ({{ legal_jurisdiction }}).

2. Local-first product

Deskline runs on your Windows PC. Time tracking records, project/task metadata, configuration, and screenshots (when enabled) are stored under your user profile (AppData). An online account is not required to track activity.

3. What may be stored on this device

  • Foreground application names and window titles used for classification
  • Optional website hints derived from browser titles
  • Session durations, idle/activity signals, and categories you set
  • Optional screenshots when that Pro/trial feature is enabled
  • A local dashboard password hash and recovery code hash if you set them
  • License status cache (key metadata) after Pro activation

4. What we do not do by default

  • We do not sell your tracking data
  • We do not upload activity logs or screenshots to {{ company_name }} servers
  • We do not use advertising trackers inside the local dashboard
  • We do not keylog, read the clipboard, or access the microphone

5. License and payment checks

If you purchase Deskline Pro, payment is processed by Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record). Deskline may contact Lemon Squeezy solely to activate or validate a license key. That traffic does not include your activity history or screenshots.

Optional RDP vision (Pro, off by default) sends a cropped image of the Remote Desktop window to a vision API that you configure with your own API key, only after explicit consent, and only while an RDP client is in the foreground. Frames are not stored as Deskline screenshots for this feature; uncertain results keep the generic “RDP · host” label. This does not replace local session timing for focus %.

6. Screenshots and sensitive content

Screenshots may include anything visible on screen. You control enabling, blur, retention, and deletion. Employer use on shared machines requires appropriate notice and lawful basis under applicable workplace law.

7. Third-party network requests

The UI may load fonts or favicon helpers when online. Optional outbound requests (favicon lookups, license validation) originate from your machine.

8. Your choices

  • Pause tracking, disable screenshots, export (Pro), or clear local data from Settings
  • Contact {{ support_email }} with privacy questions

9. Governing law

This policy is intended to be interpreted under the laws of the {{ legal_jurisdiction }}, to the extent applicable to local software.
