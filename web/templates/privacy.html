Privacy Policy

This draft describes how Deskline handles information on your device. It is a product template, not formal legal advice. Last updated: {{ copyright_year }}.

1. Who we are

Deskline is provided by {{ company_name }} ({{ legal_jurisdiction }}). Contact: {{ support_email }}.

2. Local-first product

Deskline is designed to run on your Windows PC. Time tracking records, project/task metadata, configuration, and screenshots are stored locally under your user profile (AppData) on this machine. Deskline does not require an online account to operate.

3. What may be collected on this device

  • Foreground application names and window titles used for activity classification
  • Optional website hints derived from browser titles
  • Session durations, idle/activity signals, and productivity categories you set
  • Optional screenshots of the screen when that feature is enabled
  • A local dashboard password hash if you set one

4. What we do not do by default

  • We do not sell your tracking data
  • We do not upload activity logs or screenshots to {{ company_name }} servers as part of normal operation
  • We do not use advertising trackers inside the local dashboard

5. Screenshots and sensitive content

If screenshots are enabled, images may include anything visible on screen (documents, chats, passwords typed into visible fields, etc.). You control retention and can delete screenshots from the dashboard. Blur and retention settings are available in Settings.

6. Security

Access to the local dashboard can be protected with a password stored only on this device. Physical or remote access to your Windows account may expose Deskline data. Keep your OS account secured.

7. Third-party services

The dashboard may load fonts or favicon helpers from the network when online. Those requests are not used to sync your activity history. Optional outbound requests (for example favicon lookups) happen from your machine, not from a {{ company_name }} cloud backend.

8. Your choices

  • Pause tracking, disable screenshots, or clear local data from Settings
  • Contact us at {{ support_email }} with privacy questions

9. Governing law

This policy is intended to be interpreted under the laws of the {{ legal_jurisdiction }}, without regard to conflict-of-law rules, to the extent applicable to a local software product.
